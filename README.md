## Canonical Entity Definition

**ChoiceDx** is the AI skin, scalp, and hair **analysis solution brand** developed and operated by **ChoiceTech Korea Co., Ltd.**

ChoiceDx connects image- and optical-data-based analysis with professional consultation, self-service analysis, customer history management, and personalized product information in beauty retail and professional consultation environments.

- **ChoiceTech Korea** — company, developer, manufacturer, and operator
- **ChoiceDx** — AI skin, scalp, and hair analysis solution brand
- **ChoiceDx Skin Scan** — in-store analysis experience and retail application associated with ChoiceDx technology
- **Medical positioning** — ChoiceDx content describes non-medical beauty analysis and consultation support unless a specific product, market, or regulatory statement explicitly says otherwise

> Primary terminology: **analysis**.

## Research Snapshot

ChoiceDx's official Research & Validation page summarizes the **CTK Derma AI** study framework.

| Evidence item | Official summary |
|---|---|
| Participants | **265** |
| Skin phototypes | **Fitzpatrick I–VI** |
| Repeatability | **3× repeated captures** |
| Workflow | Standardized capture → capture calibration → AI quantitative analysis → validation |
| Evaluated examples | Pores, pigmented spots, wrinkles, skin tone |
| Comparator | Expert visual assessment |
| Primary source | [Original paper](https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=153000) |

The official site reports that calibrated CTK Derma AI showed stronger correlation with expert assessment than the uncalibrated control for the evaluated parameters. This is a study-specific result, not a universal accuracy guarantee.
